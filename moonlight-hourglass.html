Four studies. Four journeys through time.

Four research reports land on the Mist River Health Bureau's desk, each claiming a discovery. With a mysterious Moonlight Hourglass, you can travel to the moments when each study's data were really created, and see what went wrong.霧川鎮衛生局收到四份研究報告，每份都宣稱有重大發現。藉由神秘的月光沙漏，妳可以穿越到每項研究資料真正產生的時刻，看看哪裡出了問題。

You'll face a cross-sectional study, a case-control study, a retrospective cohort and a prospective cohort. Each has its own way of travelling through time, and its own biases and limitations. Every wrong answer costs a grain of time sand.妳將面對橫斷性研究、病例對照研究、回溯性世代研究與前瞻性世代研究。每種設計穿越時間的方式不同，偏差與限制也不同。每答錯一次，就會耗費一粒時光砂。

About 20 minutes · Four reports plus a final challenge · All characters, places and data are fictional.約 20 分鐘 · 四份報告＋最終挑戰 · 所有人物、地點與數據皆為虛構。
